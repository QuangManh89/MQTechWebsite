# Technical SEO Skill

## Workflow
1. Confirm the production host and canonical domain; do not guess between www and apex.
2. Inspect robots.txt, sitemap, redirects, status codes, canonical tags and noindex behavior.
3. Check unique titles/descriptions, headings, internal links, image alternatives and page rendering.
4. Validate structured data against visible content and official schema/search documentation.
5. Check mobile usability, performance and duplicate/parameter URLs.
6. Test staging and production indexing controls separately.
7. Report findings with URLs and reproducible evidence.

## MQTech rules
- Domain `mqcomputech.com` is proposed until purchase and DNS are verified.
- Do not add fake LocalBusiness fields.
- Do not block important assets needed for rendering.
- Keep staging/private admin pages out of search.
- Do not promise ranking outcomes.

## Outputs
A prioritized audit with severity, evidence, fix, verification and remaining risk.
