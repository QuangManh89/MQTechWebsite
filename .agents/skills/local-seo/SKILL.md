# Local SEO Skill

## Workflow
1. Confirm business name, real address if public, contact channels, hours, services and actual service area.
2. Distinguish a storefront from a service-area business; use accurate business profile settings.
3. Map search intent to genuinely useful service pages.
4. Keep NAP and business facts consistent across approved channels.
5. Plan internal links, local proof, helpful FAQs and conversion paths.
6. Define measurable SEO checks without promising rankings.

## MQTech context
- Target: Buôn Ma Thuột and nearby wards within an approximate 15 km radius, subject to actual service capacity.
- Exact address, phone, hours, prices, and domain ownership are not yet verified.
- Use placeholders or explicitly marked unknowns until verified.
- Do not generate thin doorway pages for every ward.
- Do not fabricate reviews, local partnerships, credentials, prices or customer stories.

## Outputs
- Local intent map.
- Page recommendations and rationale.
- Verified-information checklist.
- Local business profile and structured-data requirements.
