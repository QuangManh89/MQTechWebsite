# SEO and Content Rules

- Optimize for the actual service area and real business operations.
- Use the proposed domain `mqcomputech.com` only after ownership and DNS are verified.
- Do not invent a physical address, opening hours, prices, reviews, or business claims.
- Do not publish doorway pages or near-duplicate pages for every ward.
- Create individual service pages only when they provide distinct, useful information.
- Keep visible business information consistent across the website and business listings.
- Add LocalBusiness/appropriate structured data only with verified properties; structured data must match visible page content.
- Do not promise rankings or guaranteed traffic.
- Prioritize helpful content, crawlability, performance, accessible navigation, and accurate contact information.
- Use a real canonical host after domain configuration is verified.
